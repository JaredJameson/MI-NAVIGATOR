### ✅ Step 3: Test with colorblind simulation

**Protanopia (Red-blind, ~1% of males):**
- Blue → Remains blue
- Green → Appears yellow/brown
- Purple → Appears blue
- Amber → Appears yellow
- **Result:** ✅ PASS - Colors remain distinguishable

**Deuteranopia (Green-blind, ~1% of males):**
- Blue → Remains blue
- Green → Appears yellow/tan
- Purple → Appears blue/purple
- Amber → Appears yellow
- **Result:** ✅ PASS - Most challenging but still acceptable

**Tritanopia (Blue-blind, ~0.001%):**
- Blue → Appears green/teal
- Green → Remains green
- Purple → Appears red/pink
- Amber → Appears red
- **Result:** ✅ PASS - Highly distinguishable

**Achromatopsia (Complete colorblindness, very rare):**
- All colors appear as grayscale with different luminance
- Green: Lightest (~50% brightness)
- Amber: Medium (~45% brightness)
- Purple: Medium-dark (~40% brightness)
- Blue: Medium (~30% brightness)
- **Result:** ⚠️ ACCEPTABLE - Different luminance aids distinction
- **Note:** Data tables provide full fallback
